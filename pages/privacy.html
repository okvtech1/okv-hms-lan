Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us") handle personal data when you use this website, download the free trial, buy a licence, request installation or contact us. It is written to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). Please also read our NDPA Notice and Cookie Policy.

1. Who we are

OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us") is the data controller for the personal data described here.

OKV Technology Consults
Email: technologyokv@gmail.com
WhatsApp: +234 810 414 1138
Website: www.okvtechnology.com

2. What we collect

  • Forms you submit: name, hospital or business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, city or state, the type of facility, your message, the number of computers and preferred date for installation requests.
  • Payment information: the payment reference, amount, date and the receipt image or PDF you upload for bank transfers. Receipts may show your bank name, account details and name. If you pay with Paystack, Paystack collects your card or bank details on its own secure page. We never see or store your full card number; we receive only the transaction reference, amount, status and email.
  • Licence information: the Installation ID of your copy of the software, the version, and the activation code we issue to you.
  • Technical data: our website host and the services we use may log your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

3. What we do not collect

We do not receive patient records. The OKV Hospital Management System stores patient, clinical and billing data on your own computer. It is not sent to us. We see that data only if you choose to send us a backup file for support, and then only to help you and under your instructions.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use trusted service providers to run our business, and they process data on our instructions:

  • Google (Google Workspace: Sheets, Drive, Gmail and Apps Script) to store submissions and receipts and to send emails.
  • Paystack to process online payments.
  • Website hosting and content delivery providers to serve this website.
  • Optional messaging providers (for example an SMS provider) if we send you a text message.

We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect our legal rights.

6. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some providers store data on servers outside Nigeria. Where that happens we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards, as the NDPA requires.

7. How long we keep data

We keep form submissions and licence records for as long as you are our customer and afterwards for as long as needed for support, disputes and the periods that accounting, tax and other laws require. Receipts and payment records are kept for those legal periods. You may ask us to delete other data.

8. Security

We limit access to our records to authorised staff, protect our dashboard with a password and session controls, and keep payment secrets private. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will act on it and notify the people and authorities the NDPA requires.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to our use of it; give you your data in a portable form; and withdraw consent. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within the time the law allows.

10. Children

Our website and services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
