Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

We offer a free 12-day trial so you can test the Software with your own data before paying. This policy explains refunds and cancellations for OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us").

1. Software licence

Because you can try every feature free before you buy, licence payments are not refundable once an activation code has been issued, except in these cases:

  • the activation code does not work and we cannot fix the problem or issue a working code within 7 days of your report;
  • you were charged more than once for the same licence (we refund the extra payment);
  • you paid but we did not deliver a code within 48 hours of confirming your payment and you ask for a refund instead.

2. Installation services

You may cancel or reschedule an installation without charge if you tell us at least 48 hours before the agreed time. After work has started, charges for the time already spent apply. If we cannot complete the agreed work for reasons on our side, we refund the part not delivered.

3. How to ask

Send your request with your payment reference to the contact details below. We reply within 3 working days.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method or bank account within 7 to 10 working days. Paystack or bank fees may apply as the provider decides.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under Nigerian consumer protection law.
