Features

Everything your hospital needs

Built for clinics, private hospitals, maternity homes and diagnostic centres.

Pt

Patient records

Register once and get a patient ID and printable card. Allergies show in red on every page.

Ap

Appointments

Book by doctor and date. One click checks the patient in and opens the visit.

Dr

Doctor consultation

Vitals, notes, diagnosis, lab orders and prescriptions on one screen. Print prescriptions.

Lb

Laboratory

Test list with prices and reference ranges. Results go straight to the doctor.

Rx

Pharmacy and stock

Dispense queue, automatic stock reduction and low-stock alerts.

Wd

Wards and beds

Admit, track beds, keep nursing notes and a medication chart, discharge with an automatic bill.

Bl

Billing and receipts

Invoices built from the visit, part payments, cash, POS or transfer, printable receipts.

Hm

HMO and insurance

Set each HMO's share, print claim statements and mark claims settled.

Rp

Reports and Excel export

Daily collections, outstanding balances, top diagnoses, CSV export for Excel.

Sc

Safe and private

Role-based access, an audit log of important actions, one-click backup.

Of

Works offline

Runs on your own computer over your office network. No internet needed day to day.

Pd

PDF manuals per role
